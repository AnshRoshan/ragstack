# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be technical, be direct, be kind. Critique code and ideas, not people.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith; engineers disagree about retrieval strategies for good reasons.
- Keep discussions on-topic and evidence-based — benchmarks over vibes, including in issues.
- Respect that this is a local-first project: do not pressure others into cloud services.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing others' private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of community discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this policy and may
ban contributors for repeated violations. Report issues to **ianshroshan@gmail.com**.
All complaints are reviewed and handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
